Nio Jin is a designer and artist working across graphic design, digital experiences, and interactive media.
Their practice moves between visual, sonic, and spatial forms, with a particular interest in how an experience unfolds through image, interface, and time.

Their work spans independent and collaborative contexts, including projects in design, technology, and research. They based in New York and holds a BFA in Communication Design from Parsons School of Design.
