Venti

The handmade mozzarella and oversized sandwiches already had a voice: direct, generous, and made at the counter.

Concept study. The restaurant did not move forward with the project.

Something specific was already there.

Ordering pages and social accounts explained how to buy, but none of them gave the food one place to set the tone. The scale of the sandwiches, fresh mozzarella, and counter-service energy became the identity rather than decoration added afterward.

Make the food immediate. Keep the practical choices easy.

The concept opens with proportion and texture, then moves directly into what to order, how the mozzarella is made, catering, hours, directions, and the restaurant’s existing delivery links.

The result

A direct, food-first website system built around proportion, texture, and quick practical choices.
