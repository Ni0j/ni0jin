Selected workMonta Tea · Jersey City

Monta Tea

Fruit, tea, and visible preparation suggested something quieter than a delivery listing: a daily ritual made carefully by hand.

Concept study. The restaurant did not move forward with the project.

The menu showed variety. The photographs suggested a world.

The public pages made ordering possible, but their images of fruit, botanicals, texture, and preparation hinted at a more complete atmosphere. The concept keeps the utility while giving the ingredients and pace room to communicate.

Organize the menu as an experience, not a spreadsheet.

Drinks are grouped into fruit and vegetable combinations, handmade traditions, and original brews. Pickup ordering, hours, and directions stay close while larger visual pauses give Monta Tea a recognizable rhythm.

The result

A calm, ingredient-led website with ordering, hours, and directions kept close.
