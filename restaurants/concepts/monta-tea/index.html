Our story

Tea for everyday life.

Monta carefully selects its ingredients and prepares each drink by hand, bringing Eastern tea traditions into the easy rhythm of a Jersey City café. We believe in flavor that feels fresh, familiar and worth returning to. Monta is more than a drink. It is an expression of Eastern living aesthetics, carefully made for everyday moments.
