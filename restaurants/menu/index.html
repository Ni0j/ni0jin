03

House rules

Please read
Revision & scope

Express includes one consolidated revision round; Custom includes three rounds. A revision refines the agreed direction. Replacing the concept, audience, main structure, or agreed functions is a change of direction and needs a new quote. Changes beyond the included Express round are quoted before work.

Publishing & outside costs

Basic delivery can use a Nio-provided temporary/project URL. A custom domain, paid hosting, and third-party subscriptions are separate from the website price. Domain costs are charged at actual price and agreed before purchase.

Invoice & payment

This menu prepares an order request, not a charge. I review your scope and email a formal invoice. A 50% deposit paid within 24 hours reserves your slot. The remaining balance is due before launch or final file delivery.
