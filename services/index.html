N°3

N°3 is a design practice that takes its cues from a good restaurant. You work directly with the person making your design, and every commission is made to order.

Its founder, Nio Jin, graduated with honors from Parsons School of Design. A website audit led her into an AI startup, where she encountered both the possibilities of automated design and the judgment it still leaves to people.

A three-hour conversation at a meetup took her to a cognitive science conference and into a perception lab. Through technical support and discussions about how people see and make sense of things, she brings another field’s questions back into her design practice.
