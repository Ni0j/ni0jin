Nio Jin

After graduating with honors from Parsons School of Design, Nio Jin followed her questions about design into an AI startup.

What began as a website audit became a firsthand encounter with AI’s possibilities, its blind spots, and the judgment design still demands.

A three-hour conversation at a meetup opened another path, through a cognitive science conference and into a perception lab.

Contributing technical support and joining its discussions gave her new ways to think about how people see and make sense of things.

She brings these perspectives back into design.

Technology, perception and visual practice inform one another, shaping how she approaches each project.
